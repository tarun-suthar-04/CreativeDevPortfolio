## 1. Creative Identity

I'm Tarun Suthar, an AI Creative Artist and Visual Content Creator
exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, visual design,
and digital storytelling.

I create AI-generated visuals, artistic portraits, geometric
illustrations, editorial posters, advertising concepts, and cinematic
short-form videos. My creative process combines AI-powered tools with an
understanding of composition, color theory, typography, image
manipulation, and visual storytelling.

From developing distinctive visual identities and consistent characters
to transforming ideas into engaging images and motion content, I enjoy
experimenting with different styles, techniques, and creative
possibilities.

My work focuses on turning concepts into compelling visual experiences
--- combining technology, imagination, and design to create distinctive
digital content.

**Creative philosophy:** I create visuals. I experiment with ideas. I
bring imagination to life.

## 7. Positioning Guidance

Present the work as a combination of **creative concept development,
AI-assisted visual production, design understanding, and refinement**
rather than positioning the role as prompt writing alone.

Emphasize the ability to develop a visual direction, guide AI tools
toward that direction, evaluate generated results, and refine the output
into finished creative content.

Avoid claiming professional experience, client results, software
mastery, or numerical achievements unless those details are
independently verified and added to this file.
